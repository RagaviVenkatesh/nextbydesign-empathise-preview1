One day workshop agenda

AI Spark Sprint is a proposed Module 1 learning offer. It includes six facilitated hours plus 90 minutes of breaks. It does not promise field validation within a single day. The legacy workshop catalogue remains unchanged.

Time | Activity | Output

09:30–10:00 | Intake context, boundaries and research permissions | Shared challenge and scope

10:00–10:45 | Screen 1 assumption and stakeholder mapping | Prioritised hypotheses and participant plan

10:45–11:00 | Break | 

11:00–12:00 | Screen 2 interview questions and neutral follow-ups | Editable interview guide

12:00–12:45 | Screen 3 scripted roleplay and peer feedback | Labelled practice record

12:45–13:45 | Lunch | 

13:45–14:45 | Screen 4 passage selection on permitted existing or demo material | Traceable passage records

14:45–15:00 | Break | 

15:00–16:00 | Screen 5 manual synthesis and review | Practice map and hypotheses

16:00–16:45 | Screen 6 gaps, limitations and learning baseline | Learning portfolio and research plan

16:45–17:00 | Application commitments and close | Named next action

Preparation and staffing

Facilitator prepares an accessible challenge, pseudonym conventions, example sources and a permission discussion. Participants work in small teams with interviewer, observer and reviewer responsibilities. Use the field kit rubric for feedback; it is a coaching aid, not an accreditation instrument.

Deliverables and limits

Assumption map, stakeholder plan, interview guide, practice transcript, evidence-linked practice synthesis and learning baseline. If real permitted sources are used, any research claim still requires explicit review; synthetic material never becomes a verified interview.

Two day discovery workshop agenda

Deep Dive is a proposed 12-hour facilitated Module 1 offer with recruitment completed before the workshop. Interview slots, stakeholder access and recording permissions must be confirmed separately. Without participants, substitute a learning outcome and a collection plan rather than promise validated findings.

09:30–10:00 | Context, permissions and intake review

10:00–10:45 | Assumptions and stakeholder coverage

10:45–11:00 | Break

12:00–12:45 | Rehearsal and field plan

12:45–13:45 | Lunch

13:45–15:15 | Scheduled interviews or field observation

15:15–15:30 | Break

15:30–16:30 | Scheduled research and source preparation

16:30–17:00 | Debrief and gaps

Day two time | Activity

Each day contains 360 facilitated minutes and 90 break minutes. Manual transcription preparation may take additional effort; overnight automated processing is not included in the local pilot.

Three day discovery and review agenda

Mastery is a proposed 18-hour facilitated Module 1 offer. Use the first two days of the discovery agenda, then add six facilitated hours to address gaps and deepen review. Three days do not guarantee representative coverage or a fixed number of personas.

Day three time | Activity | Output

09:30–10:00 | Review unanswered questions | Focused gap list

10:00–10:45 | Refine research questions and recruitment | Targeted collection plan

10:45–11:00 | Break | 

11:00–12:45 | Additional scheduled interviews or observations | New permitted primary sources

12:45–13:45 | Lunch | 

13:45–15:15 | Review new passages and revise synthesis | Updated supported claims

15:15–15:30 | Break | 

15:30–16:30 | Inspect contradictions, limits and baseline decision | Recorded review rationale

16:30–17:00 | Executive research package and next owner | Reviewed handoff or research plan

Fieldwork contingency

If stakeholders cancel or evidence remains inadequate, preserve the actual findings and explain the gap. Do not fill quotas with synthetic interviews. Lock a learning baseline or defer the research baseline where appropriate. A limited exploratory baseline requires a clear rationale for any acknowledged gaps.

Post workshop operation

Teams can add sources and revise synthesis in the same local project, then lock a new baseline. Weekly drift digests, live call integrations and ongoing subscriptions are future product phases. They require real operational ownership, monitoring and processing permissions before activation.
