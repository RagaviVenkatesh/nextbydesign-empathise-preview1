PARTICIPANT OPENING SCRIPT
We are exploring [topic] to understand recent experiences. This is not a sales call
or a test of your performance. You can skip questions or stop at any time.
With your permission, we will [take notes / record] and use [describe intended use].
Access will be limited to [people], retained until [date], with withdrawal requests
handled through [contact]. May we proceed? May we record, if applicable?
Adapt this script to your actual permissions and organisation policy before use.

INTERVIEW GUIDE
1. Tell me about the last time you [specific activity].
2. What triggered it? Walk me through each step.
3. What tools, people or workarounds did you use?
4. Where did the process become difficult? Can you describe a specific example?
5. What happened as a result? How did you respond?
6. Is there something important we have missed?
Use silence and neutral follow-ups. Avoid pitching, predicting purchases or
introducing the solution you hope participants will endorse.

OBSERVATION CHECKLIST
Record context, date, participant pseudonym and consent scope.
Capture sequence, tools, interruptions, handoffs and observed workarounds.
Separate exact words, observed behaviour and your interpretation.
Ask permission before collecting photos or documents. Remove unnecessary identifiers.
Do not infer emotion, intent or a contradiction without contextual review.

FACILITATOR REVIEW RUBRIC
Score each criterion 0 absent, 1 partial, 2 adequate for the stated scope.
Question quality: recent specific behaviour, neutral language, useful follow-ups.
Traceability: exact source passages, source type, pseudonyms and context.
Coverage: relevant stakeholder groups and critical assumptions considered.
Interpretation: observations distinguished from hypotheses and inferred feelings.
Limits: conflicting evidence, missing groups and next research actions disclosed.
Total /10 guides feedback only. It is not a validated maturity or accreditation score.

PARTICIPANT TASKS
